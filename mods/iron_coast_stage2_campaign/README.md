# Iron Coast II: Black Tide

Enable **Iron Coast II — Black Tide** in the game's Mods screen. Dependency
selection brings in all eight missions, their individual bosses, the shared
coastal enemies, the three-track soundtrack and two optional shop weapons.
Apply the selection at the main menu, then select Nightbreak Inlet in the
Hangar's mission menu. Normal pilot saves retain the campaign and package pins.

The individual mission, boss, enemy and weapon mods can also be enabled
separately. Removing a required mod shows the dependent packages before the
selection changes. Existing Stage 1 and original campaigns remain available.

The aggregate adds its own campaign; it does not override existing definitions
or grant equipment. Railburst Cannon and Depth Charge are normal optional
purchases. Fire with Ctrl, drop owned bombs with Space and cycle selectable
weapons with Alt or Shift. The shop explains each weapon's targeting and role.

This package's new briefing was created using built-in imagegen. Its saved
prompt, unchanged generated master and provenance are in `art/`. Re-export
the palette SD and 4× HD canvases with `python3 build_assets.py` (Pillow).
Gameplay, music and generated sound effects live in the individual dependencies.
