# Iron Coast — Mission 4: Stormwall

API 1 package, version 1.0.1. Enable it in Mods at the main menu and apply the selection. The normal dependency selection also enables `iron_coast_scout`, `iron_coast_turret`, `iron_coast_boss_4`, `pulse_cannon`, `seeker_swarm`, `ion_lance`, `iron_coast_music`.

Mission `iron_coast_mission_4:mission_04`: **Iron Coast 4 — Stormwall**. Destroy four required turrets and Stormwall Arsenal. Required ground objectives remain reachable; escaping optional aircraft never counts as a kill. Elapsed time alone does not finish the mission.

Scheduled spawn totals for Training / Rookie / Veteran / Elite are **35 / 50 / 65 / 84**. These are enabled scheduled entries, not simultaneous live-enemy counts. Groups can overlap when earlier enemies survive.

Align the forward guns with required targets while moving around incoming shots. If carried, select Field Repair or coolant with Cycle and use Special: they restore 25 or 30 energy respectively and consume a real item. Shop purchases and refills still cost credits.

## Equipment supplied by the mission

A temporary Pulse Cannon trial is supplied when needed and capacity allows. Hold Fire to use it alongside the forward guns; the temporary item is returned after the mission.
